
EMAIL IOC EXTRACTION OUTPUT

1. email_id
   - Unique identifier of the email file.

2. sender
   - Sender email address(es).

3. receiver
   - Receiver email address(es).

4. subject
   - Email subject.

5. reply_to
   - Reply-To email address(es), if present.

6. urls
   - URLs extracted from email body/content.

7. domains
   - Domains extracted from URLs and email addresses.

8. ips
   - IP addresses found in email content.

9. attachments
   - Attachment filename and content type.

10. security_headers
    - Received
    - Authentication-Results
    - DKIM-Signature
    - Received-SPF
    - Return-Path
    - Message-ID

11. suspicious_indicators
    - Suspicious patterns detected during extraction.
    - Examples: javascript, script_tag, embed_tag, base64_content


IOC PIVOTING OUTPUTS

ioc_relationships.json
- Connects Email -> IOC
- IOC type can be URL, domain, or IP

ioc_pivot.json
- Connects IOC -> related Email IDs

graph_data.json
- Contains nodes and edges for visualization.
- Example relationship:
  Email -> Domain -> Related Emails
